# ADR 0007 - Casing circularity as the product angle; verified-only impact ledger

Status: accepted (2026-10-22)

Michelin's TreadVision (announced March 2026) applies AI to casing inspection in retread plants. TreadOps frames
each tyre photo as a casing-intake decision: retread intake, expert inspection, or end-of-life material
recovery. Impact uses Michelin's published truck/bus figures (50 kg less raw material per MICHELIN Remix retread;
2.3 kg CO2 per kg raw material, an internal cradle-to-gate LCA factor) and counts **only inspector-verified
retread dispositions**. Model recommendations are shown separately. Every figure is labelled ESTIMATE with
its source; nothing here is presented as a measured LCA or as Michelin production data.
