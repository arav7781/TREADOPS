# Photo capture guidance

## Lighting
Use even, diffuse light. Strong shadows or a dark workshop push the brightness descriptor down and the quality screen will escalate the photo. Avoid flash glare on wet or glossy rubber.

## Focus and distance
Hold the camera steady and close enough that cracks are visible. Motion blur lowers the blur-variance descriptor; a blurred photo cannot be auto-cleared.

## Framing
TreadOps letterboxes the whole frame to 224 x 224 without cropping, so the full image is analysed. Fill the frame with the tyre region of interest and photograph sidewall, shoulder and tread separately for a full assessment.

## Privacy
Uploaded photos are stored without EXIF metadata (GPS and device data are removed at ingest). Avoid including number plates or people in the frame.
